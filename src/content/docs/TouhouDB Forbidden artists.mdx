This page contains list of artists whose entries are not allowed to be created on TouhouDB.
Please use custom artists if any of these people are attached to a song in an auxiliary, non-musical manner (e.g. as an illustrator).

For **existing** artist entries, see the [[content removal guidelines]].

## Artists who have requested their entries not to be added to the database

- Retsugo / Kimi / Mifloat (has allowed their songs and albums to be added, but not their artist page)
