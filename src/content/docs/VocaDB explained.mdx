VocaDB is a structured _database_ for organizing voice synth music.

VocaDB supports advanced and efficient search queries, and is pluggable into [[Services that make use of VocaDB APIs|other websites and services through the API]].

[[Songs]], [[Artists]] and [[Albums]] are considered as the "main" entry types.

The "secondary" entry types are [[Song lists]], [[Tags]], [[Events]], [[event-series|Event Series]] & [[Venues]]

Site features such as user accounts and discussion topics are also "entry types" in a _technical sense_.

A more complete entry type relationship is visualized below:

![Entry type graph](public/images/entry_type_graph.png "Entry type graph")

Before creating entries, make sure that they [[Content policy|belong to the database]].

[[Content removal guidelines]] describe our general policy for removing entries and information.

If you spot mistakes on entries, you can leave an [[Entry reports|entry report]] or try fixing the issue on your own.
