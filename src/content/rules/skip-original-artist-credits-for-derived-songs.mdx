excerpt: On derived songs, do not add artists from the original song entry unless the artist is directly involved.
---

If the original artist is not directly involved with the derived version, the artist credit should not be duplicated from the original entry.

For example, a song entry for a cover of a [DECO\*27](https://vocadb.net/Ar/45) song should not include [DECO\*27](https://vocadb.net/Ar/45) as the artist of the cover entry, nor should it includes the supporting producers, the animators,[^eia] the illustrators,[^eii] and other artists from the entry of the original song, unless it is listed as an artist that is direcly involved of the cover.

Note that producers that are involved on the derived version should still be added. For example, [self-covers](https://vocadb.net/T/391) and [self-remixes](https://vocadb.net/T/392) would have the same artist from the original song entries, but it is valid since they are directly involved on the creation of the cover/remix.

[^eia]: even if the cover uses the same music video

[^eii]: even if the cover uses the same thumbnail or artwork
